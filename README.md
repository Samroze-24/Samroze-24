### 🔹 Fake News Detector

- Bilingual fake news detection for English and Roman Urdu text.
- Shows confidence score and explains its decision with influential words and sensational-language signals.
- **Stack:** Python, Flask, Scikit-learn
- **Link:** https://github.com/Samroze-24/fake-news-detector

### 🔹 Pakistan Scholarship Matcher

- Matches students with scholarships based on field, CGPA, budget, and country.
- Transparent match score with reasons, deadline sorting, and filters.
- **Stack:** Python, Flask, Pandas
- **Link:** https://github.com/Samroze-24/pakistan-scholarship-matcher

### 🔹 House Price Predictor

- Estimates house prices from city, area, rooms, and location tier.
- Shows price range and which features influenced the result most.
- **Stack:** Python, Flask, Scikit-learn
- **Link:** https://github.com/Samroze-24/house-price-predictor

### 🔹 Sales Forecasting Dashboard

- Forecasts sales from historical CSV data with 7, 14, and 30-day horizons.
- Category filters, charts, and downloadable forecast results.
- **Stack:** Python, Flask, Pandas
- **Link:** https://github.com/Samroze-24/sales-forecasting-dashboard

### 🔹 Movie Recommender

- Content-based recommendations with similarity scores and reasons.
- Search, genre filters, and a surprise-me feature.
- **Stack:** Python, Flask, Scikit-learn
- **Link:** https://github.com/Samroze-24/movie-recommender

### 🔹 Customer Churn Predictor

- Predicts churn risk for single customers or in bulk via CSV upload.
- Risk levels, top risk factors, and feature importance.
